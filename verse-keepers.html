Welcome
How to play
  • Study first. Use flash, blanks, scramble, or typing until the verse feels familiar.
  • Take the Verse Test. Each test mixes 4 different ways to prove the verse. Pass every challenge to lock it in and unlock the next one.
  • Grow your Faith Journey. Practice earns XP, titles, a daily streak bonus, and weekly quest rewards.
  • Win the Monday ceremony. Whoever mastered the most verses last week gets the podium, fireworks, and a champion badge.

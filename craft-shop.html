how it works
  • Pick an order off the chalkboard.
  • Make it — drag beads onto cords, hooks, clips and rings, or stamp and doodle stationery.
  • Pack it — wrap, tissue, ribbon, a seal and a little note.
  • The closer it is to what they asked for, the bigger the tip.
  • Open the shop each real day for a streak and a punch-card gift.
  • Play for 10 minutes to start golden hour — double coins for five minutes.
  • Hearts raise your shop rank. 3-star streaks and daily chalkboard wishes pay extra.
  • Share a piece from your shelf — friends can open the link and see it.
  • In the town square, visit other shops, send them an order, or ask to be partner stores.
  • On day one, pick a shop cat. Pet them, buy cat furniture, and they may wander back with rare beads and coins. Write a motto on the window.
  • No timers, ever. Leftover orders wait for tomorrow.
